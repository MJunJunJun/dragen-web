01 — Features

The island is the menu.

No floating panels. Every action lives on something you can touch: a treasure chest, a market stall, a castle, a battle gate.

Hatch and raise

Place eggs on the Hatching Altar, watch them crack open, then feed and level up your dragons in their habitats.

Hands first

Touch, hold and pinch. Buttons are 3D tokens you press with your fingertips. Controllers work too.

Mixed reality

Your island sits on your real desk through passthrough. Move it, turn it and scale it with both hands.

3v3 dragon battles

Pick a team of three and fight across the table. Charge god powers and aim them by touching an enemy dragon.

Play with friends

Visit friends' islands, send gifts, text and voice notes, and challenge them to live battles with voice chat.

Make it yours

Dress your avatar with outfit sets and accessories, and plant gardens that keep your dragons fed.

02 — How it plays

Three moves to start.

01

Place your island

Look at your desk and set the island down. It stays anchored where you put it.

02

Touch to play

Reach out and press what you want: an egg, a dragon, a chest, a gate.

03

Hatch, raise, battle

Grow your team, take on stages, then call a friend over for a rematch.

03 — Living world

Weather rolls over your island.

Rain and lightning fall from the clouds, snow settles on the cliffs, and some nights the moon turns red.
